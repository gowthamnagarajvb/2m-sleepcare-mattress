7 Factors To Ensure Restful & Healthy Sleep

Research reveals that sleep is an active biological repair state. Follow these 7 pillars established by sleep ergonomists to optimize your circadian rhythm and spinal health.

1
Natural Rhythm & Time: Follow your inner clock. Early risers and night owls should sleep right when tiredness peaks.
2
Room Climate: A thoroughly aired bedroom (~18-20°C) ensures your body receives sufficient oxygen without catching chills.
3
Diet & Digestion: Avoid heavy meals 3-4 hours prior. Caffeine and alcohol are sleep robbers; choose warm milk or herbal tea.
4
Pre-Bedtime Rituals: Reading a book or winding down daily turns the brain steadily into night-time regenerative mode.
5
Darkness & Silence: A sleeping person still senses ambient photons and noise. Use eye masks or separate partner sleeping zones if snoring.
6
Textiles & Covers: Breathable, hypoallergenic covers and duo-covers adjust flexibly to changing seasonal temperatures.
