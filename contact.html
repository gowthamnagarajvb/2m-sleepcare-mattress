Pocket Springs (e.g. Pristine & Platino): Feature individual barrels of steel coils enclosed in fabric sleeves. Each spring reacts independently to body contours, completely eliminating partner roll-together and motion transfer.

Bonnell Springs (e.g. Elegant & Premium): Use an interconnected hourglass coil structure laced with cross-wire helicals. They offer time-tested durability, buoyant resilience, and firm orthotic back support at a lower cost.

We highly recommend the 2M Orthocare 6" or the 2M Platino 8" Healthy Spine series. The Orthocare features a 4-inch firm high-density foam base coupled with 2 inches of 100% pure natural latex and Spine Care Rubberised Coir. This multi-zone structure gives lumbar support to the 4th and 5th vertebrae while cushioning pressure points.
