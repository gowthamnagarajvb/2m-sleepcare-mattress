Mattress Buying Guidance

Six Important Questions When Selecting A Mattress

Make an educated decision based on your health, physique, and sleep habits.

1

Do you have back problems?

Choose our Orthocare 6" or Platino 8" Healthy Spine series engineered with firm high density foam and pure latex.

2

Cervical or lumbar spine area?

Targeted pelvic base support (4/5 lumbar vertebrae) and shoulder relief zones ensure spine straightness.

3

Head, neck & shoulder support?

Correct pillow elevation combined with point-elastic mattress contours eliminates morning stiffness.

4

Body proportions & BMI?

Our firmness scale matches individual BMI so you neither sink too deep nor rest on an unyielding board.

5

Air circulation & perspiration?

Vertical and horizontal air channels prevent humidity and heat traps, keeping sleep naturally cooler.

6

Reflux, asthma, or dust allergy?

Self-quilted fabrics are resistant to dust mites, bacteria, and allergens, promoting healthy respiration.
